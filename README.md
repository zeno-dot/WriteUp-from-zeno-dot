# WriteUps from zeno-dot (EN)

Hello. This is my collection of TryHackMe room walkthroughs and other CTF writeups.

Everything here is what I completed myself: recon, web enumeration, brute force, reverse shells, privilege escalation and whatever else came up on the way. Each writeup is a live process with commands and screenshots, not a retelling of theory. At the end of each one I leave my opinion about the room: what worked for me, what did not and who it is for.

Why all this. First, for myself: I do it once, write it down, and a month later I do not relearn it, I just open the page. Second, for people who are only starting: it is easier to understand where to look and what to try through someone else's walkthrough than to collect hints from comments.

Reading the writeups on the site is the better option: https://zeno-dot.github.io/WriteUp-from-zeno-dot/ — everything is formatted and easy to browse. This repo holds the source; the full list of writeups lives in index.md and on the site. If you find a mistake or have a question, open an issue or a PR.
